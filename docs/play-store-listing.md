**Full description** (max 4000 chars)
```
T9 App Dialer is a fast, minimalist app launcher that brings back classic T9
keypad typing. Press number keys the way you did on an old phone — 4-3-5-5-6
for "GMAIL" — and your apps filter instantly as you type.

It does one thing well: open apps quickly, with zero clutter and zero tracking.

FEATURES

• T9 keypad search — type app names on a classic 2-9 keypad (2=ABC, 3=DEF…).
  Smart matching by name start, word start, and substring, filtered live as
  you type.
• Light & dark themes — switch instantly with a long-press.
• Icon pack support — use your favourite installed icon packs.
• Movable container — position the dialer anywhere on screen.
• Resizable container — shrink or expand it to fit your hand.
• Quick app actions — long-press any result to open its App Info or its Play
  Store page.
• About dialog — version info and project link.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• No internet permission — the app cannot send your data anywhere.
• No ads, no analytics, no crash trackers, no third-party SDKs.
• No Android permissions requested at all.
• Works fully offline. Your settings stay on your device.

REQUIREMENTS

• Android 6.0 (API 23) or newer.
• About 15 MB of storage.

T9 App Dialer is open source. Privacy policy:
https://officialdad.github.io/t9-app-dialer/privacy.html
```

  > into Google Play's Families policy and its extra obligations. The app
