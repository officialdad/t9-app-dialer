Privacy Policy — T9 Dialer

App package com.t9dialer · Effective 21 June 2026

T9 Dialer is a minimal, offline app launcher with T9 predictive search. Your privacy is simple to describe because the app is built not to touch it.

Data we collect

None. T9 Dialer does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal or device data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting services. The app contains no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Network access

The app declares no internet permission and makes no network requests. It cannot send your data anywhere because it has no way to reach the network. The only outbound actions are standard Android links you tap yourself — opening an app's system "App info" screen, or opening a Play Store / GitHub page in your browser.

Permissions

T9 Dialer requests no Android runtime or sensitive permissions. To show your installed apps for searching, it uses Android's package-visibility declaration (<queries>) for launchable apps and icon-pack themes. This is a manifest declaration, not a grantable permission, and it exposes nothing about you to us — the app reads the list of launchable apps locally on your device and never sends it anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The app saves only your own settings (such as the selected theme and icon pack) in local app storage on your device. This never leaves the device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

T9 Dialer collects no data from anyone, including children. It is safe for use by all ages in that respect.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date.
